# Assets

`public/images/skin-pile.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for
this project. It depicts a fictional product; it is not a photograph of an actual KFC
offering. No reference project's content or runtime state was copied.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset: square hero product photo for a humorous KFC Skin Piles concept website. A large classic red-and-cream striped fried chicken paper bucket overflowing with an absurd mountain of ONLY thin curled crunchy golden fried chicken skin, absolutely no meat, no drumsticks, no bones. Bucket front has bold condensed red lettering "SKIN PILES" and small "KFC". Rich tactile golden crags and delicate paper-thin crispy edges, beautiful appetizing commercial studio food photography with hard editorial flash, warm cream seamless background, subtle ground shadow, a few scattered crispy flakes, centered bucket in full view with generous breathing room. Red, warm ivory, golden amber. High-end fast-food campaign with a playful deadpan aesthetic. No extra props, no humans, no extra labels or slogans.

Barlow Condensed and DM Sans are self-hosted under `public/fonts`; their SIL Open Font
License files are included there. Font binaries were downloaded from Google Fonts on
2026-09-08. Visitors make no Google Fonts or other third-party asset requests.
